School discipline: NELS:88 with SES and test scores

Source: R/data-disc2.R
disc2.Rd

The same 2,000 NELS:88 eighth graders as disc, in the same order, with continuous predictors: the base-year socioeconomic status composite (ses), reading and mathematics test scores from 1988 (grade 8), and the reading score from the 1990 follow-up (grade 10). One row per student. The other variables are recodes with readable names: being sent to the office for misbehaving, sex, home language, own bedroom, parents' education and race/ethnicity.

Usage

disc2

Format

A tibble with 2,000 rows and 15 variables.

male

Male. Values: 0 = female; 1 = male. Type: integer. Coded 0/1 (the source Stata file carried outdated 1/2 labels from the unrecoded item). Same 2,000 students as disc, in the same row order.

notengl

Home language is not English. Values: 0 = English (including students who skipped the item); 1 = another language. Type: integer. From NELS item BYS22, the language usually spoken at home: 1 = codes 2 to 13; 0 = English (197) or a legitimate skip (1,560). Coded 0/1 (the source Stata file carried outdated 1-13 language labels).

bedroom

Has own bedroom. Values: 0 = no; 1 = yes. Type: integer. Coded 0/1 (the source Stata file carried outdated 1/2 labels). Same as disc$bedroom.

osentoff

Times sent to the office for misbehaving. Values: 0 = never; 1 = once or twice; 2 = more than twice. Type: integer. Same as disc$osentoff (NELS item BYS55A).

ses

Socioeconomic status composite. Units: composite score. Type: double. NELS base-year composite BYSES, built from parents' education and occupations and family income.

reading1

Reading score, 8th grade (1988). Units: standardized score (mean about 50, SD about 10). Type: double. NELS base-year composite BY2XRSTD.

math1

Math score, 8th grade (1988). Units: standardized score (mean about 50, SD about 10). Type: double. NELS base-year composite BY2XMSTD.

reading2

Reading score, 10th grade (1990). Units: standardized score (mean about 50, SD about 10). Type: double. NELS first follow-up composite F12XRSTD. Most NAs (215 of 218) are students who did not complete the test.

fath_coll

Father graduated from college. Values: 0 = no; 1 = yes. Type: integer. Derived from disc$bys34a: 1 = codes 5 to 7 (college graduate, master's, Ph.D./M.D.).

fath_ed

Father finished high school. Values: 0 = did not finish high school; 1 = high school graduate or more. Type: integer. Same as disc$fath_ed.

moth_coll

Mother graduated from college. Values: 0 = no; 1 = yes. Type: integer. Derived from disc$bys34b: 1 = codes 5 to 7 (college graduate, master's, Ph.D./M.D.).

moth_ed

Mother finished high school. Values: 0 = did not finish high school; 1 = high school graduate or more. Type: integer. Same as disc$moth_ed.

minority

Racial/ethnic minority. Values: 0 = White; 1 = Asian, Hispanic, Black or American Indian. Type: integer. Derived from NELS bys31a; NA for the 24 nonrespondents.

race

Race/ethnicity. Values: Asian; Hispanic; Black; White; American Indian. Type: factor. Factor built from NELS bys31a (Asian = Asian or Pacific Islander), as in disc. The American Indian level was added in v1.0.0 for 68 students who were NA before; the 24 nonrespondents are NA.

sentoff

Ever sent to the office for misbehaving. Values: 0 = never; 1 = once or more. Type: integer. Recode of osentoff (1 = codes 1 and 2); same as disc$sentoff.

See codebook("disc2") for this table as a data frame.

Source

National Center for Education Statistics. (1988-1990). National Education Longitudinal Study of 1988 (NELS:88), base year and first follow-up (Public-use data files). U.S. Department of Education. https://nces.ed.gov/surveys/nels88/

Teaching extract of 2,000 NELS:88 students prepared for UC Berkeley GSE courses (EDUC 293A/275B). Provenance: verified.

Details

Coding. All the recodes are 0/1 except osentoff (0 = never, 1 = once or twice, 2 = more than twice) and the factor race, which has the same five levels as in disc, with Asian as the first (reference) level. fath_ed and moth_ed mean the parent finished high school; fath_coll and moth_coll mean the parent graduated from college. notengl is 1 for the 220 students whose home language is not English; the 1,560 students who legitimately skipped the NELS home-language item are coded 0, together with the English speakers. minority is 1 for Asian, Hispanic, Black and American Indian students alike.

Scores. reading1 and math1 (grade 8, 1988) and reading2 (grade 10, 1990) are standardized test scores with mean about 50 and SD about 10, so a coefficient per 10 points is roughly a coefficient per SD. ses is the NELS base-year SES composite, built from parents' education and occupations and family income; it is on a standardized scale (here from -2.9 to 1.9, SD 0.8).

Missing data. reading2 is NA for 218 students, 215 of whom did not complete the 1990 test; reading1 and math1 are NA for 77 and 75 students, and ses for 1. As in disc, parents' education is unknown for 282 fathers and 208 mothers. These values did not change in 1.0.0.

Version 1.0.0. As in disc, race gained the level American Indian for 68 students who were NA before; the 24 nonrespondents are NA. minority already counted these 68 students as 1. No other values changed.

Suggested analyses.

  • logistic regression of sentoff on continuous predictors such as ses, math1 and reading1, with odds ratios per unit or per 10 points;

  • comparing ses with the parents' education dummies as measures of family background;

  • linear regression of reading2 on reading1 and other predictors, a simple model of change between grade 8 and grade 10;

  • an interaction between a dummy and a continuous predictor, such as male by ses.

Ethical context

The cautions on the disc page apply here too: the outcome is the student's own report, not a school record; the race categories are those of the 1988 NELS questionnaire; and a race coefficient describes a difference without explaining it. Since version 1.0.0 the 68 American Indian students have their own race level instead of being NA, though estimates for so small a group are imprecise. Two points are specific to this file. minority merges four groups, so a single coefficient can hide differences between them. And SES and test scores differ by group in these data (mean ses is about 0.1 for White and -0.6 for Hispanic students), so adding them to a model changes what a race or minority coefficient compares.

disc for the same students with the original NELS items; nels_data for 299 variables on the same students; codebook() and list_datasets().
