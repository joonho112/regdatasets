Teachers' math knowledge and first-grade math gains

Source: R/data-instruction.R
instruction.Rd

Mathematics gains of 1,190 first graders in 312 classrooms and 107 schools, with characteristics of the students, their first-grade teachers and their schools. The data come from the Study of Instructional Improvement (SII), an observational study analyzed by Hill, Rowan and Ball (2005), and are the classroom data of West, Welch and Galecki's (2006) book Linear Mixed Models. Each row is a student.

Usage

instruction

Format

A tibble with 1,190 rows and 12 variables.

girl

Girl. Values: 0 = boy; 1 = girl. Type: integer. Called sex in the original classroom data (WWGbook::classroom).

minority

Minority student. Values: 0 = no; 1 = yes. Type: integer.

mathkind

Math score, spring of kindergarten. Units: score points. Type: integer.

mathgain

Math gain, kindergarten to first grade. Units: score points. Type: integer. Spring of first grade minus spring of kindergarten; can be negative.

ses

Socioeconomic status. Units: composite score. Type: double.

yearstea

Teacher's years of teaching experience. Units: years. Type: double. Classroom level (first-grade teacher).

mathknow

Teacher's math content knowledge. Units: scale score. Type: double. Classroom level; based on a 30-item scale, higher = better knowledge. Missing for 27 of the 312 classrooms (109 students).

housepov

Neighborhood poverty rate. Units: proportion (0 to 1). Type: double. School level: share of households in the school's neighborhood below the poverty level. Stored as a proportion, not a percentage.

mathprep

Teacher's math preparation. Units: score. Type: double. Classroom level; based on the number of math content and methods courses taken.

classid

Classroom ID. Type: integer. 312 classrooms, nested in schools.

schoolid

School ID. Type: integer. 107 schools.

childid

Student ID. Type: integer.

See codebook("instruction") for this table as a data frame.

Source

West, B. T., Welch, K. B., & Galecki, A. T. (2006). Linear mixed models: A practical guide using statistical software. Chapman & Hall/CRC. Data from the Study of Instructional Improvement (Hill, Rowan, & Ball, 2005, American Educational Research Journal, 42(2), 371-406). https://cran.r-project.org/package=WWGbook

The classroom data of West, Welch and Galecki, as instruction.dta in Rabe-Hesketh and Skrondal (Stata Press), via the UC Berkeley EDUC 275B course files; also on CRAN (WWGbook). Provenance: verified.

Details

Design. Students are nested in classrooms (1 to 10 students per classroom) and classrooms in schools (2 to 31 students per school). yearstea, mathknow and mathprep describe the classroom's teacher and are constant within a classroom; housepov is constant within a school. Because the study is observational, regression coefficients describe associations rather than causal effects of teachers or schools.

Coding. mathgain is the spring first-grade score minus the spring kindergarten score (mathkind), so it can be negative (39 students). housepov is stored as a proportion (0.012 to 0.564), not a percentage. girl (1 = girl) is called sex in the original classroom data. mathknow is missing for 27 of the 312 classrooms (109 students); the other variables are complete. Version 1.0.0 changed no values; it corrects the v0.2.0 help page, which misdescribed the study.

Suggested analyses.

  • one-way ANOVA of mathgain across schools, factor(schoolid);

  • simple and multiple regression of mathgain on mathkind, ses, minority and teacher variables such as mathknow;

  • multilevel structure: compare ordinary least-squares standard errors for classroom- and school-level predictors with those from a model that allows for clustering.

See also

hsb_sub and gcse also have students nested in schools. The same classroom data are on CRAN as WWGbook::classroom, where girl is called sex. codebook() gives the variable table as a data frame, and list_datasets() lists the other datasets.
