General Social Survey 1998

Source: R/data-gss_1.R
gss_1.Rd

Responses of all 2,832 people interviewed in the 1998 General Social Survey (GSS), the survey of U.S. adults run by NORC at the University of Chicago. Each row is a respondent, with attitudes to abortion and cohabitation, political views, religion, sexual partners, own income, marital status, schooling, parents' schooling, age, sex and race. The file is a teaching extract of unknown authorship in which several GSS items were recoded or combined into scores.

Usage

gss_1

Format

A tibble with 2,832 rows and 16 variables.

abortion

Abortion attitude score (0 to 7). Units: scale score. Type: double. Undocumented composite of unknown authorship; its 172 fractional values suggest a 0-7 item count prorated over the items answered. Higher scores go with more liberal views in these data.

cohabit

Cohabitation attitude score (2 to 10). Units: scale score. Type: integer. Undocumented composite of unknown authorship. Higher scores go with more liberal, less religious and younger respondents, so higher probably means more accepting of cohabitation.

income

Respondent's own income bracket. Values: 1 = under $1,000; 2 = $1,000 to $2,999; 3 = $3,000 to $3,999; 4 = $4,000 to $4,999; 5 = $5,000 to $5,999; 6 = $6,000 to $6,999; 7 = $7,000 to $7,999; 8 = $8,000 to $9,999; 9 = $10,000 to $12,499; 10 = $12,500 to $14,999; 11 = $15,000 to $17,499; 12 = $17,500 to $19,999; 13 = $20,000 to $22,499; 14 = $22,500 to $24,999; 15 = $25,000 to $29,999; 16 = $30,000 to $34,999; 17 = $35,000 to $39,999; 18 = $40,000 to $49,999; 19 = $50,000 to $59,999; 20 = $60,000 to $74,999; 21 = $75,000 to $89,999; 22 = $90,000 to $109,999; 23 = $110,000 or more. Type: integer. GSS RINCOM98: the respondent's own income (not household income), in unequal brackets. Per the 1998 NORC GSS archive, 798 of the 983 NAs were inapplicable (mainly people without earnings); the source file collapsed all missing reasons to plain NA.

conserv

Political views. Values: 1 = extremely liberal; 2 = liberal; 3 = slightly liberal; 4 = moderate; 5 = slightly conservative; 6 = conservative; 7 = extremely conservative. Type: integer. GSS POLVIEWS; higher = more conservative.

educat

Years of schooling. Units: years. Type: integer. GSS EDUC, highest year of school completed; 20 = 8 or more years of college.

male

Male. Values: 0 = female; 1 = male. Type: integer.

maed

Mother's years of schooling. Units: years. Type: integer. GSS MAEDUC; 20 = 8 or more years of college.

paed

Father's years of schooling. Units: years. Type: integer. GSS PAEDUC; 20 = 8 or more years of college.

marital

Marital status. Values: 1 = married; 2 = widowed; 3 = divorced; 4 = separated; 5 = never married. Type: integer.

partnrs5

Sex partners in the past 5 years. Values: 0 = no partners; 1 = 1 partner; 2 = 2 partners; 3 = 3 partners; 4 = 4 partners; 5 = 5 to 10 partners; 6 = 11 to 20 partners; 7 = 21 to 100 partners; 8 = more than 100 partners. Type: integer. GSS PARTNRS5; codes 5 to 8 are ranges (v0.2.0 wrongly said 8 = 8 or more), so treat it as ordinal, not a count. Per the 1998 NORC GSS archive, the 495 NAs include 34 respondents who answered 1 or more without a number (GSS code 9); the source file collapsed all missing reasons to plain NA.

respage

Age. Units: years. Type: double. GSS AGE; 89 = 89 or older. The 4 respondents with no answer were given the mean age (45.55693), the only non-whole values.

race

Race. Values: 1 = White; 2 = Black; 3 = other. Type: integer. GSS RACE.

black

Black. Values: 0 = no; 1 = yes. Type: integer. Dummy for race = 2.

othrace

Other race. Values: 0 = no; 1 = yes. Type: integer. Dummy for race = 3 (neither White nor Black).

relosity

Religiosity score. Units: scale score. Type: double. Undocumented composite of unknown authorship, with mean 0 and no NAs. Higher scores go with having a religious affiliation and with conservative views in these data.

religion

Religious preference. Values: 1 = Protestant; 2 = Catholic; 3 = Jewish; 4 = none; 5 = other. Type: integer. Recode of GSS RELIG: Protestant includes Christian, inter-/nondenominational and Orthodox Christian; other includes Buddhism, Hinduism, other Eastern, Muslim and Native American.

See codebook("gss_1") for this table as a data frame.

Source

Davern, M., Bautista, R., Freese, J., Herd, P., & Morgan, S. L. (2026). General Social Survey 1972-2024 (Data file). NORC at the University of Chicago. https://gss.norc.org/

Teaching extract of the 1998 GSS cross-section, of unknown authorship, via the UC Berkeley EDUC 275B course files. Provenance: probable.

Details

Recoded variables. income is the respondent's own income (GSS RINCOM98) in 23 unequal brackets, not household income. conserv is GSS POLVIEWS (higher = more conservative), religion regroups GSS RELIG into five categories, and respage gives the mean age, 45.56, to the 4 respondents who did not report their age. partnrs5 codes 5 to 8 are ranges (5 to 10, 11 to 20, 21 to 100 and more than 100 partners), so treat it as ordinal, not as a count. race, marital and religion are integer codes: use factor() or, for race, the dummies black and othrace.

Scores of unknown construction. abortion (0 to 7), cohabit (2 to 10) and relosity (mean 0) are composites built by an unknown party, and no record of their items or scoring has been found. The 172 fractional values of abortion suggest an item count prorated over the items answered. In these data, higher abortion and cohabit scores go with more liberal views, and higher relosity scores go with having a religious affiliation and with conservative views. Report them as scores of unknown construction, not as validated scales.

Missing data. 55% of cohabit, 35% of income, 34% of abortion and 28% of paed are NA, and only 348 rows are complete on all 16 variables. Per the 1998 NORC GSS archive, 798 of the 983 missing incomes were "inapplicable" (mainly people without earnings; the source file collapsed all missing reasons to plain NA), so a model that uses income describes people with earnings. lm() and glm() drop incomplete rows by default (listwise deletion): a regression of relosity on factor(religion), maed, respage, black and othrace uses 2,371 of the 2,832 rows. Rows 285 and 349 are identical on all 16 variables; whether they are two respondents or one duplicated record is not known.

Version 1.0.0 changed no values. It corrects the survey year and the meanings of income and partnrs5 given in the v0.2.0 documentation.

Suggested analyses.

  • logistic regression of a dichotomized score, e.g. abortion >= 4, on conserv, educat and relosity, with odds ratios;

  • dummy variables for religion, marital or race;

  • comparing listwise deletion with other ways of handling missing data, such as missing-value indicators or multiple imputation.

See also

codebook() for the variable table as a data frame, and list_datasets(), e.g. list_datasets(topic = "logistic"), for other datasets suited to logistic regression.
